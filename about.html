Our Heritage

A Legacy of Luxury
Since 1998

Founded in 1998 by the visionary hotelier Étienne Moreau, Grand Élysée Hotel was born from a singular dream: to create a sanctuary where luxury transcends the ordinary. Nestled in the heart of Paris, the hotel was built in a magnificent Belle Époque building that had stood as a beacon of architectural beauty for over a century.

Over more than two decades, we have welcomed royalty, world leaders, celebrated artists, and discerning travellers who seek nothing but the finest. Our philosophy is simple — that true luxury is not about opulence alone, but about the warmth of genuine care and the art of anticipating every desire.

Today, Grand Élysée stands as one of Europe's most celebrated luxury hotel brands, with a reputation built on integrity, excellence, and an unwavering commitment to creating unforgettable experiences.

Our Journey

Milestones That Shaped Us

The Grand Opening

Étienne Moreau opens Grand Élysée Hotel in a restored Belle Époque mansion, welcoming the first 80 guests with 40 luxurious rooms.

1998
2003

First 5-Star Rating

Grand Élysée receives its first official 5-star rating and is inducted into the prestigious Leading Hotels of the World collection.

The Grand Expansion

A major renovation doubles the hotel's capacity to 180 rooms, adds the Élysée Spa, and launches our award-winning rooftop restaurant.

2009
2015

Michelin Star Dining

Our flagship restaurant "Le Jardin d'Élysée" is awarded its first Michelin star, cementing our culinary reputation on the world stage.

Green Hotel Certification

Grand Élysée becomes one of the first luxury hotels in Europe to achieve Platinum Green Globe certification for sustainable operations.

2020
2025

Digital Luxury Experience

Launched our state-of-the-art digital concierge platform and completed a full renovation of the Royal Penthouse and Presidential Suites.
